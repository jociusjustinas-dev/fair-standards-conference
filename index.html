About the conference

Fair licensing across
interconnected markets

Fair Standards, Global Impact brings together judges, academics, industry leaders, and policymakers to examine how best to support the widespread adoption of technical standards through fair licensing of standard essential patents across increasingly interconnected global markets. From video streaming to aggregate royalties, from injunctive relief to regulatory intervention, this conference will cover a wide range of the most pressing topics surrounding SEP policy in 2026.

  • Streaming Standards
  • Fair Enforcement
  • Aggregate Royalties
  • Policymakers

Keynote 1 · 10:00 – 10:30

A View from the Bench: SEP Adjudication in Brazil

Brazil has become a key SEP jurisdiction, with courts facing questions of injunctive relief, royalties, and reach. This is a rare view from the bench — and lessons that travel well beyond Brazil.

  • Injunctive relief
  • Royalty determination
  • Jurisdictional reach

Keynote 2 · 13:45 – 14:15

FRAND Adjudication and Rate Setting Before the UPC

The UPC has quickly become a pivotal forum for SEP litigation in Europe. This conversation looks at how the court is approaching FRAND, rate setting, and what that means for the licensing landscape.

  • FRAND compliance
  • Rate setting
  • Injunctive relief

Panels

Four conversations
on SEP policy

Streaming Standards — Fair Licensing in Focus

Video streaming sits at the sharp end of SEP licensing — codecs, protocols, and a patent stack that touches billions of consumers.

  • Who bears the cost of the patent stack?
  • How should royalties be allocated across the value chain?
  • How has the licensing model changed over the years?
  • Which video codec standards to implement, and how?
  • Can current models scale fairly as the sector expands?

Fair Enforcement: Proportionate Remedies in SEP Disputes

Injunctive relief remains one of the biggest hurdles for standards adoption. Practitioners, jurists, and scholars compare when remedies stay proportionate — and when they tip into hold-up.

  • When should an injunction be granted?
  • When does relief become disproportionate?
  • What do recent rulings and Commission findings show?
  • Can proportionality be applied consistently across the EU?

Aggregate Royalties — How to Create a Workable Mechanism?

Knowing the total royalty cost of a standard is critical — and still one of the thorniest problems in SEP licensing.

  • Who calculates the aggregate royalty?
  • What methodology should be used?
  • How do you account for patents of varying strengths?
  • Can a top-down approach reconcile licensors and licensees?
  • Are industry-led solutions enough, or is regulation needed?

From Debate to Action — What Should Policymakers Do?

SEP policy is now a live agenda worldwide. This panel moves beyond diagnosis — a call to action for fair marketplaces.

  • Which interventions are needed, and which are working?
  • Which risk unintended consequences?
  • Should policymakers step into royalty determination?
  • How can policy serve fair standards, not gatekeepers?
